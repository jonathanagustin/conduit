## Evidence fidelity

Each capability is marked against the confidence vocabulary in
[`../DEPLOYMENT-EVIDENCE.md`](../DEPLOYMENT-EVIDENCE.md): **Primary** (directly observed - here the
recovered authorization graph), **Derived** (mechanically follows from primary - a gated platform
capability), **Corroborating** (a second channel consistent with the claim - the circumstantial
tier, e.g. a GraphQL seam), **Unknown** (the corpus does not prove it - here the Hub wire
protocol), and **Local choice** (a reconstruction decision, never a claim about Apollo).
