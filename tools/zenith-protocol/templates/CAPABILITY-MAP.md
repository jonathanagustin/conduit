# Capability map — the standing ledger of SOLVED / BLOCKED / OPEN

Read this **before any discovery, recovery, or investigation attempt**. It is the memory that stops the
work re-deriving something already solved, blocked, or ruled out. Keyed by hypothesis (H#). Write back
in the same change that taught you something.

Two rules for what you write here:
- **A negative needs its tier.** "Not found" means "absent from *this* artifact/channel", never "absent
  from reality". A wrong negative is worse than a gap: a gap invites a look, a false negative forbids one.
- **Record the diagnosis, not just the symptom.** The exact instrument that failed (the tool + the error,
  the permission, the tier) is what makes the next attempt cheaper.

| Hyp | claim / method | status + evidence |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | _describe the hypothesis and how you tested it_ | **OPEN / SOLVED / BLOCKED** — what you found, with a pointer to the evidence/commit |

Status vocabulary: **SOLVED** (proven, with a repeatable gate) · **BLOCKED** (with the exact failing
instrument + a formulated path past it) · **OPEN** (a live lead). When you resolve an OPEN to SOLVED,
update the row and cite what proved it.
